<h1 align="center">👋 Welcome to Nistra</h1>
<p align="center">Creators of <b>Demy</b> — streamlining academy management in Peru</p>

We are **Nistra**, a software startup founded by Software Engineering students at UPC.  
Our mission is to transform the way educational academies manage their operations.

## 🚀 Our Product: Demy
**Demy** is a web platform that streamlines academy management by centralizing key processes:
- Student enrollment
- Attendance tracking
- Class scheduling
- Payment tracking for students and teachers

## 🎯 Mission
Revolutionize the administrative management of educational academies in Peru by offering a platform that optimizes enrollment, attendance, scheduling, inventory, and payments.

## 🌎 Vision
Become leaders in the digital transformation of educational academies in Peru, ensuring efficiency, transparency, and accessibility through innovative technology.
